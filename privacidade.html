Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

What we collect. When you connect your TikTok account, we receive your TikTok display name, avatar and an access token. We also store the videos and captions you choose to publish, until they are published or removed.

How we use it. Only to show which account is connected and to publish or schedule the videos you select. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Storage. Data is stored on the server where TikVidsTok runs and is not used for advertising or analytics.

Your choices. You can disconnect an account in the app, which deletes its stored tokens, and revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings. To request deletion of your data, contact us.
